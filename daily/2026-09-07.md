## 2. Molecular Déjà Vu: Digit-Level Retrieval of Published Values in Frontier Language Models

AI Importance Score：5.7 / 10

**Facts：** Audits 22 frontier models on 12 molecular regression benchmarks for verbatim retrieval of published values. Finds retrieval is widespread but benchmark-specific, with over 50% of models showing retrieval on five datasets. Reasoning level affects retrieval, with higher reasoning leading to 89% more flagged instances. Attempts to interrupt retrieval show some models still recognize transformed SMILES and original labels.

**Analysis：** Highlights a significant issue in evaluating LLMs on scientific benchmarks: models may memorize and retrieve known values rather than predict from molecular structure. This undermines the validity of benchmark results and suggests that current evaluations may overestimate true predictive ability. The finding that reasoning increases retrieval suggests that models may use memorization more when prompted to reason.

**技术看点：** Provides a methodology for detecting verbatim retrieval in regression benchmarks, which can be applied to other domains to ensure evaluation integrity.

**可能影响：** Calls into question the reliability of LLM performance on molecular property prediction and similar benchmarks, prompting the need for more robust evaluation protocols that prevent or account for memorization.

**Sources：**

- [arXiv AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05381v1)
